Find your state legislators

Use the General Assembly’s official lookup. This site never sees the address you type there.

  1. Open the official lookup. It opens in a new tab on palegis.us.14
    Open Find My Legislator
  2. Enter the street address, city and ZIP code of where you live. Only Pennsylvania addresses can be searched.14
  3. The results name the Pennsylvania House member and the Pennsylvania Senator for that address, and also list your U.S. House member and U.S. senators.14 Our campaign is about state law, so the first two are the people to write to.
  4. Write to them with our letter tool.
